# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme's project management processes and role guidance. Together, the documents describe how cross-functional projects move from an idea to delivery and continuous improvement.

## Process at a glance

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative approach with clear ownership and data-informed decisions:

1. **Initiate** — validate the need, identify stakeholders, define success measures, and decide whether to proceed.
2. **Plan** — agree on scope, backlog, responsibilities, dependencies, risks, and milestones.
3. **Execute and track** — deliver and test small increments, review progress, and address blockers.
4. **Release** — verify readiness, deploy, communicate the release, and roll back or mitigate issues if needed.
5. **Learn and improve** — capture outcomes and turn retrospectives into actionable improvements.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; the Product Manager defines outcomes and priorities; and the delivery team and stakeholders contribute to implementation, quality, and decisions.
